GEETHU'S CORNER - HOW ORDERING WORKS

---
HOW TO ORDER ON THE WEBSITE
1. Open the Menu on the home page and click "Add to cart" on the cakes you want.
2. Open the Cart, change the quantities if needed, and click "Proceed to checkout".
3. You must be logged in. New customers can create an account on the Sign up page in one minute.
4. At checkout, choose delivery or pickup, the date, the time slot and the payment method, then click "Place order".
5. The order appears on the "My orders" page as Pending until we confirm it.

DATES AND TIME SLOTS
- Orders can be placed from tomorrow up to 30 days ahead. Same-day orders are not possible on the website.
- Some dates may be closed (holidays) or fully booked. The checkout page shows which dates are not available.
- Time slots: Morning (10 AM to 1 PM), Afternoon (1 PM to 4 PM), Evening (4 PM to 8 PM).

---
PAYMENT
- Pay when you receive the cake: cash or UPI.
- There is no online payment on the website.

---
ORDER STATUS AND TRACKING
- The "My orders" page shows each order's progress: Placed, Confirmed, Baking, Ready, Delivered.
- Click "Refresh" on that page to see the latest status.

---
CANCELLING AN ORDER
- You can cancel an order yourself on the "My orders" page while it is still Pending.
- After we confirm the order, please call or WhatsApp us to cancel.

---
CUSTOM CAKES
- For special designs (theme cakes, photo cakes, shapes, special text), use the "Custom cake" page.
- Tell us the occasion, flavour, size, date and your design idea. A budget is optional.
- Custom cakes need at least 2 days' notice.
- We reply on the same page with a price, or tell you if it is not possible.
- You can send reference photos to us on WhatsApp.

---
SIZES
- Menu prices are for the standard size of each cake.
- For other sizes (0.5 kg, 1.5 kg, 2 kg or bigger), use the Custom cake page or contact us.
