ABOUT GEETHU'S CORNER

---
WHO WE ARE
Geethu's Corner is a small home bakery in Chennimalai, Erode, Tamil Nadu. Every cake is crafted with love, care and the warmth of home.

---
OUR STORY
For the past five years we have been baking cakes for birthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings.
The heart behind our bakery is Geeta, a passionate homemaker who turned her love of baking into her own business.

---
WHAT MAKES OUR CAKES SPECIAL
- No artificial colours, no preservatives and no chemical additives.
- Healthier choices such as brown sugar cakes and wheat cakes.
- Every cake is freshly baked to order.

---
WHAT WE MAKE
Cakes for birthdays, anniversaries and family celebrations, and custom theme cakes on request.
The current list of cakes and prices is on the Menu of our website.
